Advanced 802.11 Auditing.

A completely standalone offensive security toolkit. Transform your ESP32 into a modernized penetration testing platform with an embedded Single Page Application dashboard.

Airspace Scanner

Discover nearby access points, evaluate encryption strength, and map 2.4GHz topology.

Technical Execution: Temporarily drops out of promiscuous mode to execute WiFi.scanNetworks(). Parses wifi_auth_mode_t to classify cryptographic risks.

Promiscuous Prober

Silently extract BSSIDs of hidden client devices connected to a target Access Point without authenticating.

Technical Execution: Tunes radio to target channel. Hooks esp_wifi_set_promiscuous_rx_cb(). Intercepts and parses raw WIFI_PKT_MGMT frames.

Deauth Injector

Forcefully disconnect targeted devices or blanket an entire network with localized denial of service.

Technical Execution: Overrides firmware sanity checks to construct and inject raw 0xC0 IEEE 802.11 Deauthentication management frames.

Beacon Flooder

Saturate the 2.4GHz spectrum with dozens of fake Access Points simultaneously.

Technical Execution: Utilizes a dedicated FreeRTOS task to construct byte-perfect 0x80 Beacon frames. Modifies SSID payloads dynamically in memory.

AP Spoofing (Ghost)

Listen for devices searching for saved Wi-Fi networks, and instantly reflect fake APs using those names.

Technical Execution: Sniffs 0x40 Probe Requests. Extracts raw SSID byte payload and spawns responsive beacons to deceive the client OS.

Captive Portal

Perfectly clone a target network and route victims to a credential-harvesting local web server.

Technical Execution: Tears down legitimate SoftAP and clones target MAC via esp_wifi_set_mac. Initiates DNSServer to hijack UDP Port 53.

Deployment Protocol

Configure, flash, and connect to the auditing dashboard.

01

Configure Home AP

Configure your 2.4GHz Wi-Fi router or mobile hotspot with the exact Network Name (SSID): ESP32-OST and Password: Password123.

02

Connect Hardware

Plug your ESP32 board into your computer using a data-capable USB cable. Ensure browser serial permissions are allowed.

03

Flash Firmware

Click the flash button below. Upon completion, the ESP32 will reboot and automatically connect to your ESP32-OST network.

04

Access Dashboard

Find the local IP address assigned to the ESP32 by your router/hotspot and enter it into your web browser to initialize the dashboard. You can also see the IP of the ESP from the serial monitor at 115200 baudrate.

Hardware Initialization

Flash the firmware directly from Chrome/Edge using Web Serial API.
